## Uploading

**Documents → Upload** accepts PDF, images, spreadsheets and text files up to the configured size. Files are stored outside the web root and served only to signed-in members of your workspace.

## Attaching

In the composer or in campaign settings, click **Attach** and pick documents. For channels that cannot carry attachments automatically (manual sends, the browser extension), the document list is shown so you can send it yourself.

## Knowledge base

`.txt` documents uploaded under **AI Assistant → Knowledge** are chunked and indexed for the AI. Other documents are for attaching only.

## Deleting

Deleted documents move to **Trash** for 7 days.
