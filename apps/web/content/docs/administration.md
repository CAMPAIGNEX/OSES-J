## Account

Name, email, password and personal timezone. If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them here.

## Team and roles

Invite colleagues to the workspace. **Owner** and **Admin** manage settings, providers, exports and automation; **Member** works with leads, clients and the inbox.

## Company

The verified facts the AI uses. Keep them accurate; the AI never uses anything else about your company.

## Appearance

Choose the workspace **template**:

- **Classic**: clean and quiet.
- **Bauhaus Mix**: Bauhaus geometry with graffiti, mixed-media and pop-art touches.
- **Neo**: futuristic glass over a slow aurora with cyan and violet light and subtle motion.

The template applies to everyone in the workspace. Light and dark mode are personal to each device.

## Messaging

Default channel, AI mode (Manual, Copilot, Autopilot), working hours, follow-up days, messages per hour/day and minimum spacing.

## Automation

The browser extension (on/off, pairing, paired devices) and the **Services** list showing what the CNEX AI team has activated for your workspace: discovery and enrichment, the AI agent, the extension and Meta. Technical settings (provider keys, Actors, job limits) are not part of the workspace; they are managed by CNEX AI.

## Social accounts

Meta connections and their status.

## Audit log

Every important action (logins, settings changes, sends, deletions, AI decisions) with who, when and from where.

## Security in brief

- Each workspace is isolated; nobody outside it can see its data.
- Provider tokens and AI keys are encrypted at rest.
- Sessions are stored as hashes; sign-in is rate limited.
- Webhooks from Meta are signature-checked.
- Uploaded files are stored outside the web root and served only to members.
- Do-not-contact and opt-out are enforced by the server, not just the interface.
